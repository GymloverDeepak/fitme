# FITME v1.1.1

Offline Android fitness app by **Gymlover Deepak**. No account, no ads, no internet needed for its features.

## What's new
- **Midnight Titanium theme** — a new deep-navy look with titanium surfaces and electric-blue highlights across every screen. Completed sets now show green, and the next set to do is outlined in blue.
- **New app icon** — the Gym Lover Deepak logo together with the FITME name, sized for every Android launcher shape and for themed icons on Android 13+.
- **BMI calculator** — enter weight in kg and height in cm or feet and inches. Shows your BMI with the standard adult screening categories. Adult categories are not applied to under-18s or during pregnancy. BMI is a screening tool, not a diagnosis, and can be misleading for muscular athletes. Open it from Home or the Progress tab.
- **New training styles** on the Workout tab, with a "Training style" filter:
  - **HYROX-style** — Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced (running, rowing, SkiErg, sled, carries, lunges, wall balls), each with no-equipment alternatives. Not an official HYROX programme.
  - **CrossFit-style** — Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced strength, conditioning and bodyweight sessions. Technique first; no fast complex lifts for beginners.
  - **Six-pack abs** — Core Foundations and Core Progression. Ab training strengthens your core but cannot burn belly fat on its own.
  - **Fat loss** — full-body strength, walking, low-impact cardio and simple circuits, with adjustable duration. No spot reduction or guaranteed results.
  - **Women's low-impact cardio & light weights** — gentle, no-jump sessions of 20, 30 or 40 minutes, including guidance for returning to exercise.
  - **Prenatal gentle movement** — general, conservative options only, for people whose clinician has said exercise is suitable. Shows the warning signs to stop and seek medical advice. Not tailored by trimester and not a replacement for prenatal care.
- **Session guide** — the new plans show how to run each session, the warm-up, the cool-down and easier options.
- **32 new exercises** with instructions, safety notes and equipment alternatives. They show a placeholder until their demonstrations are added.

All existing plans, exercises, demonstrations, the Deadlift Planner and your saved data are unchanged.

## Install
1. Download `FITME-v1.1.1.apk` below on your Android phone (Android 7.0 or newer).
2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser or file manager for this one app.
3. Tap **Install**, then **Open**.

**Upgrading from v1.1.0:** install over the existing app. It updates in place and keeps your saved workouts, history, weights and settings.
